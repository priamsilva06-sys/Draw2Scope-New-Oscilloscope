How to use: Draw any shape in the Draw Box (it replaces on new stroke). Watch the dot trace your exact path on the Oscilloscope in real time. Toggle Sweep Mode to see your shape's Y-profile as a classic waveform. Enable sound to hear the curve as stereo audio (left = X position, right = Y position). The frequency controls both visual tracing speed and the pitch of the sound. Use sliders to rotate, scale, offset the shape live. Persistence creates phosphor afterglow effect.
